## What Happens on Cleaning Day

Our crew comes directly to your home or business and cleans your bins in place — you don't need to be there. Every cleaning includes:

- **Hot water pressure wash** — we blast the interior and exterior of each bin with high-pressure hot water to remove buildup, grime, and residue
- **EPA-registered disinfectant** — applied to kill odor-causing bacteria and harmful germs
- **Fresh deodorizer** — a final treatment that leaves your bins smelling clean and neutralizes future odors

The entire process is self-contained. Our team handles the wastewater and leaves your property tidy when the job is done.

<Note>

</Note>
