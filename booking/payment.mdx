Tega Cay Bin Blasters accepts payment through Venmo or by card via a secure Square payment page. Send your payment before or at the same time you submit your booking request — your appointment slot is not held until both are received.

<Warning>
  Your appointment is only confirmed once **both** your payment and your booking request email have been received. Sending one without the other will leave your appointment unconfirmed.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Venmo">
    Venmo is the quickest way to pay. Follow these steps after completing the booking form:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Venmo">
        Launch the Venmo app on your phone or visit [venmo.com](https://venmo.com) in your browser.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find the Account">
        Search for **@Micah_collins36** — copy the username carefully to make sure you're sending to the right account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the Exact Amount">
        Type in the exact dollar amount shown in your order summary at checkout. Do not round up or down — send the precise total displayed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the Payment">
        Add a note referencing your service address or appointment date, then tap **Pay** to complete the transaction.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Screenshot your Venmo confirmation screen — it's handy proof of payment if you ever need to follow up.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pay by Card">
    Prefer to pay with a debit or credit card? Use the secure Square payment link built right into the booking page.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Pay by Card">
        On the booking page, click the **Pay by Card** button. This opens a secure Square payment page in a new tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter Your Card Details">
        Type in your card number, expiration date, CVV, and billing ZIP code. Square uses industry-standard encryption to protect your information.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete the Payment">
        Review the amount shown — it will match your order summary — and click **Pay** to process the charge. You'll receive a confirmation from Square once the payment goes through.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      The Square payment page is hosted securely by Square. Tega Cay Bin Blasters never stores your card information.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Have a question about a charge or need help with payment? Email [tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com](mailto:tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com) and the team will get back to you promptly.
