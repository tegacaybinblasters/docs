Booking a bin cleaning with Tega Cay Bin Blasters takes just a few minutes. Before you start, have your service address and a preferred date and time ready so you can move through the form without interruption.

<Tip>
  Ready to get started? Head straight to the booking form at [tegacaybinblasters.com/#book](https://tegacaybinblasters.com/#book).
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Service and Add-Ons">
    Select the service type that fits your needs — **One-Time**, **Recurring**, or **Commercial**. Then enter the number of bins you want cleaned. Once you've set your service, choose any add-ons you'd like to include and review the order summary to confirm everything looks right before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Appointment Details">
    Fill in your first and last name, phone number, and the service address where the bins will be located. Use the optional notes field to share anything the crew should know ahead of time — for example, a gate code, a dog in the yard, or specific placement instructions for your bins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Date and Time, Pay, and Send Your Request">
    Choose your preferred cleaning date and time slot from the available options. Complete your payment — by Venmo or card through Square — and then send your booking request email to [tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com](mailto:tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com). The booking page provides quick-send links for Gmail, Yahoo Mail, and your default email app so you can fire off the request in one click. Your appointment is confirmed once both your payment and your email request are received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On your cleaning day, make sure your bins are empty and placed at the curb or somewhere easily accessible in your driveway. You don't need to be home — just leave the bins out and the crew will take care of the rest.
</Note>
