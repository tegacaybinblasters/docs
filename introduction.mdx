Tega Cay Bin Blasters makes it easy to keep your trash and recycle bins clean, sanitary, and odor-free. Whether you're a homeowner tired of smelly cans or a business that needs regular upkeep, our crew handles the dirty work so you don't have to. Every single cleaning — no matter which plan you choose — includes a hot water pressure wash of the interior and exterior, an EPA-registered disinfectant treatment, and a fresh deodorizer to leave your bins smelling clean.

## What's Included in Every Clean

No matter which service tier you select, every visit covers the full treatment: we scrub the inside and outside of each bin with high-pressure hot water, apply disinfectant to kill germs and bacteria, and finish with a deodorizer so your bins stay fresh until the next cleaning.

## Service Tiers

- **One-Time Cleaning** — Starting at $10 for one can, up to $32 for four or more cans. Perfect if you want to try the service or just need a one-off refresh.
- **Recurring Plans** — Monthly and quarterly subscriptions starting at $15/month. Save money and keep your bins consistently clean all year long.
- **Commercial Service** — Starting at $45 for businesses with higher-volume or specialized cleaning needs.
- **Add-Ons** — Customize any service with a Deep Clean (+$8), an Extra Can (+$7), or an Odor Bomb + Sanitize treatment (+$10).
