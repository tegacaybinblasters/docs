Find quick answers to the most common questions about scheduling, preparation, and what to expect when Tega Cay Bin Blasters comes to clean your bins. If you don't see what you're looking for, reach out directly — the team is happy to help.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to be home during the cleaning?">
    No — you don't need to be home at all. The crew works independently and only needs access to your bins. Just make sure your bins are placed at the curb or in an accessible spot in the driveway before your appointment window, and the team will handle everything from there.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I prepare my bins for cleaning day?">
    Empty your bins completely before the crew arrives — loose trash or bags left inside can interfere with the cleaning process. Place your bins at the curb or in a spot that's easy to reach from the street. You don't need to do any pre-rinsing or scrubbing; that's what Tega Cay Bin Blasters is here for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my bins are extremely dirty?">
    Don't worry — heavily soiled bins are a specialty. The crew uses professional-grade equipment and high-pressure hot water to break down built-up grime, odors, and bacteria. If your bins need extra attention, note it in the optional notes field when booking so the crew can come prepared.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?">
    Yes, you can reschedule or cancel your appointment by emailing [tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com](mailto:tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com) as soon as possible. Please reach out with as much advance notice as you can so the team can adjust the schedule and accommodate other customers. Include your name, service address, and original appointment date in your message.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What areas do you serve?">
    Tega Cay Bin Blasters primarily serves Tega Cay, SC and the surrounding communities. If you're unsure whether your address falls within the service area, send an email to [tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com](mailto:tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com) with your address and the team will confirm availability for your location.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will you arrive for my appointment?">
    After you submit your booking request, you'll receive confirmation of your scheduled time slot. Arrival times can vary slightly depending on the day's route and traffic, so the crew may arrive anywhere within your selected window. If you have a tight schedule, add a note when booking and the team will do their best to accommodate you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if it rains on my scheduled cleaning day?">
    Light rain typically won't affect your cleaning — the crew uses water-based equipment anyway. In the event of severe weather that prevents safe service, Tega Cay Bin Blasters will contact you to reschedule at no extra charge. Keep an eye on your email on days with forecasted storms in case you receive a rescheduling notice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up for a recurring cleaning plan?">
    Select **Recurring** as your service type on the booking page and choose how often you'd like your bins cleaned. Recurring plans are a great way to keep your bins consistently fresh without having to rebook each time. You'll be set up automatically after your first appointment is confirmed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
