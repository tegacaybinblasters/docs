Signing up for a recurring plan is the easiest way to keep your bins fresh all year long. Instead of remembering to book each time, you get placed on a regular cleaning cycle — so your cans are always clean, sanitized, and odor-free without any extra effort on your part. Recurring customers also save compared to booking one-time cleans at the same frequency.

## Available Plans

All plans include the same full-service clean: interior and exterior wash, hot water, EPA-approved disinfectant, and fresh deodorizer.

| Plan | Cans Covered | Frequency | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | 2 Cans | Every month | $15/mo |
| Monthly | 3 Cans | Every month | $22/mo |
| Quarterly | 2 Cans | Every quarter | $40/qtr |
| Quarterly | 3 Cans | Every quarter | $55/qtr |

<Tip>
The **Monthly Plan** is the best regular value for most households — your bins stay consistently clean and odor-free throughout the year, and you lock in a low recurring rate.
</Tip>

## How Recurring Scheduling Works

Once you sign up for a recurring plan, we place you on a set cleaning cycle based on your chosen frequency. You don't need to do anything — we show up on schedule, clean your bins, and you're done. You'll always receive the same thorough, professional clean every visit.

## Changing or Cancelling Your Plan

Life changes, and we get that. If you need to switch plans, adjust the number of cans, or cancel your subscription, just send an email to [tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com](mailto:tegacaybinblasters@yahoo.com) and we'll take care of it promptly.

<Note>
Add-ons such as the Odor Bomb + Sanitize or an Extra Can can be included with any recurring plan. See the [Add-Ons page](/services/add-ons) for details.
</Note>
